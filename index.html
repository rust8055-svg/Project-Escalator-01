Introduction

An escalator is a moving staircase designed to transport people between different floors of a building. It is commonly used in shopping malls, airports, railway stations and metro stations.

Aim of the Project

To demonstrate the working mechanism of an escalator and understand how electrical energy is converted into mechanical motion.

Materials Used

  • Cardboard
  • DC Motor
  • Battery
  • Wires
  • Switch
  • Glue
  • Decorative Materials

Working Principle

The escalator operates using an electric motor connected to a chain mechanism. When power is supplied, the motor drives the chain, causing the steps to move continuously in a loop. This demonstrates the conversion of electrical energy into mechanical energy.

Applications

  • Shopping Malls
  • Airports
  • Metro Stations
  • Railway Stations
  • Commercial Buildings

Conclusion

This project demonstrates the practical use of motors and mechanical systems. Escalators provide efficient transportation between different levels and are an excellent example of physics and engineering in daily life.
